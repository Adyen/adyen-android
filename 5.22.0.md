### Fixed
- Compromised [eMudhra certificates](https://emudhra.com/en-in/) no longer cause a vulnerability:
  - On Android devices without Google Play services: Drop-in and Components now block the eight [compromised eMudhra intermediate CA certificates](https://groups.google.com/a/mozilla.org/g/dev-security-policy/c/0R3XLJL6FuE/m/0XofdebrAAAJ). Devices with Google Play services were already protected by an automatic update from Google.
