### Fixed
- The "Save QR code" button in the QR code component now uses OkHttp, which blocks the compromised eMudhra certificates.
- Updated OkHttp to 4.12.1, which blocks 8 compromised eMudhra intermediate CA certificates. This protects shoppers on Android devices without Google Play services, which still trust these certificates. Devices with Google Play services are already protected by an automatic update from Google. For more information, see the [OkHttp 4.12.1 changelog](https://lysine.dev/okhttp/changelogs/changelog_4x/#version-4121).
